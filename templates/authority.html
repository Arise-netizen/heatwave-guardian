Government Heat Action Plan (HAP) Standard Operating Matrix

Actionable protocols standardized across District Disaster Management Authorities

Framework Architecture
RED ALERT PROTOCOL EMERGENCY

Triggered when daily temperatures exceed severe heatwave departure criteria (Plains ≥ 44°C) or WBGT enters Extreme Danger.

  • Mandatory work stoppage for manual outdoor labor 11 AM – 4 PM.
  • Activate emergency cooling shelters in all municipal wards.
  • Deploy municipal water tankers to slums and construction zones.
  • Put government hospital heat-wards on code-red alert.
ORANGE ALERT PROTOCOL WARNING

Triggered when temperature departure is 4.5°C to 6.4°C above normal or WBGT reaches Danger zone.

  • Enforce 30m work / 30m rest hourly shifts for laborers.
  • Open public drinking water kiosks along transit corridors.
  • Broadcast public advisories on local media and SMS.
  • Equip traffic police booths with oral rehydration packs.
YELLOW WATCH PROTOCOL ADVISORY

Triggered when temperature departure is moderate or WBGT enters Extreme Caution band.

  • Issue heat awareness bulletins to schools and workplaces.
  • Ensure school sports activities cease before 10 AM.
  • Inspect municipal piped water pressure and storage tanks.
  • Stock primary health centers with IV electrolytes.

ML Hyperlocal Layer

Downscales coarse ~25km reanalysis to localized in-situ urban microclimates ($T_{\text{local}}^{\text{ML}}$) using real-time background weather, surface imperviousness, vegetative canopy deficit, and solar loading.

🔬 Non-Medical Environmental Downscaling
ℹ️

Scientific Safety Architecture: The ML model refines the localized temperature ($T_{\text{local}}^{\text{ML}}$) by predicting the Urban Heat Island anomaly ($\Delta T_{\text{UHI}}$). All resulting Wet-Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT) values and ISO 7243 operational work-rest schedules are calculated through the transparent, deterministic biometeorological engine. The ML layer never declares medical diagnosis or mortality prediction.
