How is Thermal Stress calculated? Thermal Stress is derived from outdoor Wet-Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT) using the Liljegren et al. (2008) method. Meteorological conditions such as air temperature, humidity, wind and solar radiation are used to estimate the natural wet-bulb and globe temperatures, which are then combined as: WBGT = 0.7 × Tnwb + 0.2 × Tg + 0.1 × Ta
