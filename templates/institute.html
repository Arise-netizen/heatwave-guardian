Mandatory Shift Supervisor Protocol Checklist (Factories & Construction Corridors)

1. Potable Drinking Points

Ensure continuous, readily accessible supply of clean potable drinking water and oral rehydration salt packets located within 50 meters of the active workface, encouraging regular drinking.

2. Active Shaded Enclosures

Provide well-ventilated or misted resting tarpaulins with cross-breeze or evaporative coolers for designated 15–45 min hourly cool-down rotations.

3. First-Aid & Emergency Evacuation

Maintain trained occupational first-aiders equipped with thermal thermometers, ice towels, and a dedicated vehicle on-site for immediate hospital transfer.
