Cooling-Station Siting Recommender

Algorithmic decision-support module that evaluates candidate urban transit hubs, open-air wholesale markets, and informal labor corridors to identify optimal locations for emergency hydration kiosks, shaded rest pavilions, and misting shelters.

Decision-Support Transparency Notice:

Siting recommendations are advisory rankings generated from multi-criteria spatial formulas: $$\text{Siting Score} = f(\text{WBGT Heat Load [45%]}, \text{Distance to Hospital [25%]}, \text{Worker Density [20%]}, \text{Canopy Deficit [10%]})$$ These results assist municipal commissioners and disaster management authorities in resource allocation; they are not an authoritative statutory mandate.
